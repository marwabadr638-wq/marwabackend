Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your educational investment. Our refund policy is designed to be fair to both students and the creator.

1. General Refund Eligibility

Refunds are allowed within 7 days of the original purchase date, provided that less than 20% of the course content has been accessed or viewed.

2. Significant Consumption

If our system shows that you have accessed, downloaded, or viewed a significant portion (20% or more) of the course materials, you will no longer be eligible for a refund, regardless of whether it is within the 7-day window.

3. Bundles

For bundle purchases (such as the Tri-Therapy Bundle), refunds are only permitted if no courses within the bundle have been started. Once any content within the bundle is accessed beyond the 20% threshold, the entire bundle becomes non-refundable.

4. Technical Issues

If you are experiencing technical difficulties that prevent you from accessing the course, please contact support. Refunds due to technical issues that cannot be resolved will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
